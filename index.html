Invomile Privacy Policy

Invomile: Invoices & Mileage (formerly SimplesPro) · Effective 27 September 2026

In short: everything you put in Invomile stays on your device. There's no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. I don't collect, see, sell or share your information.

Invomile ("the app") is an invoicing, receipt and mileage app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Danniel Oliveira, a sole developer in New South Wales, Australia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app handles and how.

1. What the app stores on your device

  • Your business details: business name, contact details, tax ID (ABN or EIN), and the bank or PayPal details you choose to print on invoices.
  • Clients: names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and tax IDs you enter or pick from your contacts.
  • Invoices: line items, amounts, tax, dates, notes and payment status.
  • Receipts: the receipt photo or file, and the merchant, date, amount, tax, category and text read from it.
  • Trips (car logbook / mileage log): start and end time, start and end location (coordinates and street name), distance, whether the trip was business or personal, the purpose you type, odometer readings you enter, and your vehicle details.
  • Settings: your country, tax settings and app preferences.

All of this is stored only on your device. The app has no account, no login and no server, and it doesn't send this information to me or to anyone else. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple manages under its privacy policy.

2. Information I collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs. I don't collect usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts or crash data through the app. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to me.

3. Location (automatic mileage)

  • Location is used only for the trip logbook. Automatic trips are off until you turn them on, and are available on iPhone only.
  • When automatic trips are on and you allow "Always", the app uses low-power location signals (significant location changes, visits and an area around where you parked) to notice when you start driving. Precise GPS runs only while a drive is being checked and recorded, and stops a few minutes after you stop.
  • Only the start and end of each trip and its distance are saved. Your route is never stored.
  • To show street names, the start and end coordinates are sent to Apple's location service (under Apple's privacy policy). They are not sent to me or to any other service.
  • If you use "Record a Trip Now", location is used only until you end that trip.
  • You can turn automatic trips off in the app, or change location access at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

4. Motion & fitness

On iPhone, the app uses motion activity to tell driving from walking, so only car trips are logged. Motion data is read on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.

5. Camera, photos and files

The camera is used only when you scan a receipt. You can also pick a photo or file yourself; the app only receives the item you choose. The text on receipts is read on your device with Apple's on-device text recognition. Receipt images are stored on your device.

6. Contacts

When you tap the contact button, the system contact picker opens. The app only receives the contact you choose (name or company, and their first email address and postal address) to fill in the client. It can't read your other contacts.

7. Siri and voice invoices

If you create an invoice with Siri, Apple converts your speech to text under Apple's privacy policy. The app then reads that text on your device, using Apple's on-device model where available, to fill in the invoice. Your words aren't sent to me or any other service.

8. Sending and exporting

Invoices, reports and logbooks are created on your device. When you email, message, share, save or export them, they're handed to the app or place you choose, and that app (and the person you send it to) handles them under their own policies.

9. Notifications

Payment reminders and the free-trial reminder are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

10. Subscriptions and purchases

Invomile Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes all payments. The app only receives whether your subscription or trial is active. I never receive your name, Apple Account, card or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

11. Deleting your data

You can delete invoices, clients, receipts and trips in the app, and clear your business details in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (except copies in your own device backups). Because I don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for me to delete on my side.

12. Children

The app is a business tool and isn't directed to children under 13.

13. Your rights

Australia: I handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you have a question or complaint, contact me using the email below. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

United States and elsewhere: I don't collect, sell or share personal information, and the app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, it will also be noted in the app's release notes.
